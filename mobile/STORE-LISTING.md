Review these drafts against the final tested app before submitting. They do not replace store privacy disclosures or a published privacy policy.

## Name

Soft OS

## Apple subtitle

A calmer way to plan your day

## Google short description

Plan your days with tasks, habits, budgets, notes, and a focus timer.

## Description

Make room for your day with Soft OS, a personal planner that brings your calendar, tasks, habits, money, and thoughts together.

- Plan events and organize your to-do lists.
- Build habits and track your progress.
- Record expenses and income, and organize savings jars.
- Keep notes and a personal journal.
- Use a focus timer to make space for the task in front of you.
- Choose a theme that feels right for your day.

The core planner stores your information on your device. Account sync and the Pebble AI assistant use online services. When you use the assistant, your message and a summary of your planner may be sent to the AI service to generate a reply. Read the privacy policy for details.
